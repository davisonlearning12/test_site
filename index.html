Welcome

This is my test website made with Sitewright. 

Sitewright is a simple webpage creator that generates webpages to host on GitHub. Inspiration is from Google Sites, an easy to use website creator that hosts pages on google drive. 

Why make something that does essentially the same thing? Well it is a good question! I suppose Google Sites pages are really locked to google drive, it's difficult to get the files and make them usable in other places. Sitewright makes simple HTML websites and all the files are saved in GitHub. The HTML files can be saved, edited, shared and moved around freely.
